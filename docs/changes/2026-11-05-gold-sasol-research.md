The last completed retained Sasol closes are R300.56 on 23 October and R265.50 on 3 November, a decline of about 11.7%; 4 November was a JSE holiday. The 5 November session is intraday and is not a completed close. [Sasol published its quarterly operating metrics on 21 October](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-sens/202610210001a-s605292). The retrospective pullback replay recorded its Sasol signal on 21 October, entered at the next available close on 22 October, and exited on 27 October due to the fixed three-session horizon, net +R331.59 under the assumed cash/cost model. Its trace records trend, a 4.24% pullback, trigger, relative volume 1.1969 and stop geometry. The later decline occurred after this particular simulated trade exited; the replay does not tell us that it predicted or avoided the decline. [Sasol's dated AGM notice](https://www.sasol.com/sasol-sens/202610150026a-s604970) schedules the AGM for 13 November. Neither the old pullback rule nor this replay incorporates historical SENS text, oil or fundamentals as decision features.

Next component experiments should compare the existing rule against individually attributable changes in entry timing, momentum or trend regime, oil/FX context, event windows, stops, trailing, targets and holding horizons. A no-event/no-context baseline and genuine later-period evaluation are needed; retrospective context cannot be backdated into a prediction.
