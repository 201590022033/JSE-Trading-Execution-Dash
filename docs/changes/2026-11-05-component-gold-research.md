# Component research and gold history — 5 November 2026

M3 now has a separate versioned component evaluator and daily replay engine. The existing pinned replay and prospective dependencies were not changed: all ten hashes in the registered plan and dependency supplement matched their files. The controls include entry, trend, RSI, volume, benchmark, stop, target, trailing activation, holding horizon and sizing. The real swing snapshot remains the swing definition; box, VCP and pullback retain their existing price rules. An explicitly disabled volume filter works without inventing volume. Daily rule observations, signals, engine decisions and trades are retained in content-addressed reports and exposed through a read-only Lab endpoint. EMA21 close is combined with the chosen 3/4/5-session maximum horizon, not an unlimited EMA-only holding period.

The Yahoo NewGold (GLD.JO) capture contains 4,340 completed sessions from 2 January 2009 to 3 November 2026; the corresponding STX40 capture contains 4,446. NewGold is a Johannesburg-listed ETF in rand, not spot gold or a spot gold futures contract. The current conservative complete-session segmentation leaves 1,716 sessions in 26 individual segments; missing sessions are not invented. The loader can select older segments, and the full retained data remains available for additional segment-level studies. The recent component study uses the latest 174-session segment. Historical bar availability is reconstructed, not an archive of what was actually received on each date.

The Sasol study uses 29 December 2025 to 3 November 2026 and the current STXRES sector proxy. Eleven exploratory variants were retained as `d7188cf2c429870e650999a686bae753c62e7b1230be048d7ec868fe67d61e85`: the baseline had three trades and -R4,814.83; removing the volume filter had five trades and +R3,697.58; four-session and five-session horizons had -R9,848.85 and -R18,389.05 respectively, each on three trades. These are in-sample observations with hypothetical costs, not evidence of a profitable or causal strategy.
