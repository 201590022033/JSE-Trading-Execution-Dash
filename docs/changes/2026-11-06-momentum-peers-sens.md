# Sector peers and timed SENS in momentum research

The owner requested using the restored OST session and Moneyweb for current
announcements, and using peer companies and timed news in momentum analysis.
The separately identified 09:42 SAST public capture retained 75 Moneyweb and
342 ShareData leads, replay-verified them and delivered 417 source records to
the radar. The research observation importer produced 56 new-evidence groups;
292 reused unchanged documents. An OST browser inspection separately observed
the 08:45 TFG interim-results, dividend and board notice. The current capture
does not claim a new OST archive or complete market-wide SENS coverage.

The momentum comparison adds a matched `momentum_peers` variant using earlier
same-current-sector peer market-state observations, normalized by each peer's
own ATR. Each peer outcome must end by the target signal's decision date.
The exact peer instruments, classifications, input field receipts, sample
counts and bounds are retained. Industry differences and current (rather than
historically verified) classification remain explicit. Missing peers fall back
to fixed 2R without blocking other variants. Existing reports remain unchanged.

Exxaro (EXX) and Thungela (TGA) were added as coal/energy resource research
peers, not equivalents of Sasol's diversified chemicals business. Their
research-only identities are supported by their 2025 reports and JSE SENS:
https://investor.exxaro.com/integrated-reports2025/downloads.php and
https://www.thungela.com/investors/. Yahoo metadata checked the JSE tickers;
both Yahoo and ShareData histories were retained. The existing mapped-source
daily refresh will include them. The mapping does not alter canonical trading.

Source context is attached to each signal with original publication precision,
acquisition, source and dashboard receipt clocks. A date-only announcement is
eligible only from its actual acquisition and receipt; no precise publication
time is invented. Later or undated-clock records remain excluded. The panel
shows known recent context and separate descriptive trade subgroups; absence of
retained news is not evidence that no news existed. No event or model-generated
explanation is assumed to cause a subsequent price move. Ask the Desk receives
compact peer and source coverage summaries when present.

Source-dated notices captured after a signal also appear separately as
retrospective leads, with their actual acquisition times and an explicit label;
they do not enter the historical strategy decision. Their dates are compared
against the signal session, not the next-day reconstructed decision date. The
panel shows their trade subgroup separately from context actually known then.
Use `--all-horizons` to compare 3, 4 and 5 observed sessions while reusing a
single validated signal list for the same entry and stop settings.

Six full-window BAT-box and Sasol-pullback reports were generated and their
content and code hashes checked. BAT peers were GPL and NWL, and Sasol peers
were EXX and TGA. For the three-session replay the peer variant produced 88
closed BAT trades, net -R101,613.76, versus 73 fixed-2R trades, -R38,009.15;
the Sasol peer variant produced 125 trades, -R191,989.43, versus 117 fixed-2R
trades, +R25,078.45. Different exits can alter later fills and position overlap.
The retained context scan found 14 BAT and five Sasol source records, but no
signal in these runs had a matching retained notice known within the preceding
30 days, nor a source-dated retrospective lead in that window. This is limited
archive coverage and cannot establish that no relevant news existed.

Price-only tests continue even when peers or context are unavailable. These
are exploratory retrospective research replays with reconstructed historical
price availability and hypothetical costs, not trained or promoted strategies.
No Qwen call, new order or trading account mutation was made.
