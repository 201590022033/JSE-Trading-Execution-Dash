## Verdict

**There are not five independently operating market-segment learning loops. No Swing strategy variant is currently being autonomously refined.**

There is one shared JSE cash-share paper/research workflow, currently configured for **Sasol only**, with Top40 benchmark context. Fixed technical and shadow-policy layers record evidence beside it. A separate Hermes **news relevance** review coordinator exists; it is not a sector-specific Swing optimizer. Historical AI hypothesis research is disabled in the accepted local configuration and separately blocked for the current OST dataset by unresolved source semantics.

The design documents specify **six hypothesis families**: one general selected-share family and five specialist market/exposure families. Those are proposed research contracts, not six deployed learners. The Top5 ranking capacity, three3/4/5-session measurement horizons and multiple strategy version numbers do not create independent learning loops.

## Blockers and audit findings

1. **Segment-loop expectation mismatch.** Five/six sector or asset learners are not running. Existing context ETFs and share classifications do not supply independent training, evaluation, parameter versions or promotion decisions for each family.
2. **Swing proposal generation is deliberately disabled.** The private accepted configuration has SWING_RESEARCH_ENABLED=0. The fresh-runtime acceptance code requires it to remain off; enabling a flag alone is neither a supported local supervisor implementation nor a historical-admission fix. The legacy default proposal transport is cloud-oriented, not the new local news-draft trial.
3. **There are no matured strategy labels to refine against.** Four feature records are not four trades; two benchmark records are not additional stock candidates. The saved policy decisions have no setup, so they cannot establish entry/stop/target performance.
4. **Current data freshness labels need careful interpretation.** The dashboard says CURRENT for the uploaded dataset, but actual accepted Sasol/Top40 price sessions end16October. The19October completed session is absent from those accepted charts. The earlier01:xxSAST capture excluded it under the conservative availability rule; a fresh upload timestamp does not add a missing session or mature an outcome.
5. **R10 source-price cohorts are a different experiment.** Existing source captures can have matured gross/assumed-cost price observations while the strategy-specific feature/policy labels remain zero. R10 does not demonstrate that a trading setup occurred or worked.
6. **No automatic refinement from news.** A calibrated model, a source-draft trial, relevant article or dated calendar event does not supply learned sector/indicator weights. Applicability, later price effect and strategy profitability have separate acceptance requirements.
7. **Historical B5 and execution evidence remain unresolved.** Source adjustment/action, volume and historical-availability semantics, plus adequate verified costs/fills/slippage, still prevent a full historical/financial-performance claim. More source rows alone do not resolve those definitions.

## Recommended next work, in dependency order

1. Make the next authentic broker capture advance accepted source sessions, while preserving raw identity/acquisition and missing-session visibility. Keep source-price, feature and policy outcomes distinct.
2. Define one explicit H1 local prospective experiment around the fixed baseline, including actual eligibility/no-setup controls, source risk, independent event counts, costs and acceptance metrics. Collect qualified future evidence before selecting a refinement. Do not silently enable the blocked historical/cloud proposer.
3. Connect a bounded local Swing diagnostic/proposal/evaluation coordinator to the existing experiment registry. It should propose one change only when evidence supports it, test against the frozen baseline on a separately declared future cohort, retain negative results and leave promotion to independent acceptance. This financial loop remains to be implemented; the existing news coordinator does not substitute for it.
4. Add specialist families incrementally with exact source, benchmark, membership/exposure and vehicle contracts. Reuse one idle-controlled engine and shared immutable data, with distinct hypothesis IDs, configurations, experiments and outcomes. Five/six separate always-running processes are unnecessary.
5. Make the dashboard show each family's state—PLANNED, DATA_BLOCKED, COLLECTING, TESTING or REVIEW_REQUIRED—plus its active baseline, candidate, samples and last actual refinement. Currently the truthful last-refinement answer is **none accepted in the current local Swing lineage**.

These are audit recommendations, not newly activated milestones or trading instructions. R12 remains ACTIVE/PARTIAL.
