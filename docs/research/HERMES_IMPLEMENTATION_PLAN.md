## Swing Hermes: diagnose, propose, test, observe

**Input.** Consume immutable source receipts, dated dataset/chart versions, frozen strategy/profile decisions, eligible and rejected opportunities, paper outcomes and R10 forward price observations. Keep source-level price cohorts distinct from strategy-decision outcomes. Exclude revised endpoints and stale or ineligible data. Missing Open, calendar, actions, actual costs or volume semantics must remain visible. Historical v3/B5 admission cannot be bypassed by relabelling a reconstructed archive as prospective.

**Diagnosis.** On a slower weekly or evidence-triggered schedule, group recurring errors: late entries, false breakouts, stop-outs followed by recovery, failed recovery, missing-data vetoes, regime instability and cost sensitivity. Count the full eligible denominator and matched no-signal controls, not just selected winners. Show independent event/episode counts, unresolved outcomes and the frozen baseline. An Ollama explanation is a hypothesis, not a numerical result.

**Proposal.** Submit one falsifiable change per `ExperimentDefinition`: add, remove or replace one indicator, alter one declared parameter, or change one exit rule while freezing all other components. Explicit factorial designs are required for bundled changes. Record source and decision cutoffs, baseline commit/configuration, instrument/horizon scope, mechanism, failure condition and data version. If source semantics are insufficient, register an exploratory/prospective-only proposal rather than invoking historical AI evaluation.

**Evaluation.** Use earlier training data, a purge/embargo covering full signal-to-exit intervals across shared instruments, and an untouched later holdout. Preserve attempt budgets and rejected variants. Compare the exact frozen baseline and matched controls under the same costs, spread/slippage stress, position/exposure constraints and data quality. Report net return distribution, win rate, drawdown, turnover, sample size, independent episode count, uncertainty and regime stability. Do not select by best recent win rate or repeatedly reuse a viewed holdout as unseen.

**Forward stage.** If independent review accepts a research candidate, freeze its version and run alongside the baseline in shadow/paper on subsequently observed data. No automatic promotion to canonical ranking, M14 policy, M15 risk, demo controls or Live brokerage follows. Any future production change requires separate acceptance, explicit authority and an ADR.

## News Hermes: relevance first, impact later

**Evidence and labels.** Retain a broad retrieved-article denominator, including rejected and untriggered items. Deduplicate same-origin SENS syndication and copied stories. Preserve publisher, URL/document hash, original publication, first observed/usable time, issuer identity and article/brief relationship. The weekly brief is a lead and cannot count as a second independent publisher. Independent reviewers label relevant, irrelevant or uncertain; direct issuer versus reviewed indirect sector/commodity/FX exposure; event category; and evidence timing. A relevant article is not automatically bullish, timely or tradable.

**Applicability.** The current explicit-issuer guard remains the default. An indirect link such as oil to Sasol or gold/ZAR to a miner requires a separately versioned, human-reviewed exposure map and cited factual relationship before it can enter a candidate. Unsupported links such as Pepkor to CFR or wheat to CLS remain rejected. No model may label its own output as ground truth or use a post-move explanation as a pre-entry input.

**Relevance experiment.** Register one change at a time to alias rules, exposure mapping, retrieval, prompt, model or calibrated relevance score. Split by time and grouped event/publisher so near-duplicate articles cannot cross train/holdout. Compare against the frozen deterministic issuer matcher and current guarded scan. Report precision, recall, abstention/coverage, calibration, false issuer links and independent reviewed sample size. Keep uncertain cases abstained and the source paused until R12 acceptance.

**Impact experiment.** Only after relevance is reliable, join to dated sector/benchmark-adjusted later returns with a complete no-news/control denominator, corporate-action/cost caveats and non-overlapping event groups. Keep attention priority separate from trade/risk weight. Estimate interpretable instrument/sector/event/regime/horizon effects with shrinkage toward neutral for sparse cells. Freeze training cutoff and test later independent events; display uncertainty and do not call temporal association causation. First output is a shadow attention-weight proposal with zero trading weight.

## Cross-loop and operating controls

- Use one durable experiment ID and artifact hashes for every proposal, run, negative result and reviewer decision. Record if a news event also appears in a Swing study; prevent duplicated event/outcome evidence from being treated as independent confirmation.
- Keep lightweight import, validation, health and receipt work separate from heavy analysis. Start backtests or Ollama inference only after Windows has been idle for at least ten minutes. Use one global heavy-job lock across both supervisors and run below normal process priority so the PC remains responsive.
- Stop heavy work when the user returns, at a defined job boundary where possible. Persist the immutable input hash, reservation and checkpoint before work; an interrupted job must remain retryable under the same attempt budget, never disappear or be counted twice. On wake/restart, inspect the durable reservation and result before resuming or repeating. Keep a hard runtime bound and visible failure state. A dashboard refresh must not trigger a model or trading cycle.
- Keep raw broker exports, source bodies and model responses in private hashed local archives. The database stores compact references, lineage and bounded projections. Never store browser credentials or expose private records in public status endpoints.
- Preserve the owner’s existing paper/demo authority and all source, B5, historical AI and Live gates. A supervisor may recommend `REJECT`, `INCONCLUSIVE` or `ACCEPT_FOR_NEXT_STAGE`; an accepted next stage is not execution approval.
