# Backtest Lab redesign

19 November 2026 · B01 review draft · source revision `f0b5484`

The Backtest Lab should let you choose a share and a research hypothesis, replay a specific strategy on attributable historical data with explicit costs, and inspect the resulting trades and evidence. It should answer what was tested, how much data was usable, how the fills and costs were modelled, and how uncertain the result is.

This follows the [Radar hierarchy](HIERARCHY.md), [operator guide](OPERATOR_GUIDE.md), [UIM4 exact-handoff checkpoint](UIM4_CHECKPOINT.md), [UIM5 tool evidence](UIM5_CHECKPOINT.md), [six hypotheses](../research/SIX_SWING_HYPOTHESES.md), and the [local engine contract](../research/LOCAL_BACKTEST_ENGINE_SPEC.md). Radar retains the selected share and manual plan. The Lab takes that exact context into a historical study and leaves historical results available for inspection after the selection changes.

## What the current Lab does

The Lab blueprint primarily serves saved research and deterministic read-only views. Its only POST is `/api/v1/backtest-lab/ask`, which requests advisory text for a selected saved run. There is a separate local Radar roster POST that can queue one component-learning cycle. The Lab has no share-first submission control for an exact strategy replay. A share can appear in a saved portfolio replay even when this exact action is unavailable.

The dropdown calls `/api/v1/backtest-lab/runs?index=filenames`. It lists up to 1,000 filenames matching 64 lowercase hexadecimal characters and initially labels them `UNVERIFIED FILE`. Opening a run invokes `_read`, which recalculates its content hash, resolves each registered strategy identity and checks trace and trade attribution. The hash is the immutable result fingerprint. Keep it under Details alongside costs, modelled fills, source receipts, clocks, configuration, and the full strategy identity.

The current coverage table is hard-coded in [hypothesis_coverage.py](../../application/backtest_lab/hypothesis_coverage.py). A repository-wide tracked documentation search for the literal word `H0` produced zero matches. The six-hypothesis document begins at H1. There is no documented H0 to carry forward.

| Family | Current examples in the coverage code | Intended selection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 · Selected share | SASOL, BTI, FSR, ABSPJ | Individual relative strength, available as a hypothesis for any watched share. Existing examples remain attributed to their actual instruments. |
| H2 · Resources and commodities | SASOL | Resources breakout research |
| H3 · Industrial or global exposure | BTI | Continuation with global context |
| H4 · Banks and financials | FSR, ABSPJ | Financial-sector recovery research |
| H5 · USD/ZAR | No equity members | Currency impulse research, including price-only studies |
| H6 · Gold | NEWGOLD | Gold impulse research, with listed vehicle and reference inputs distinct |
| Telecommunications · proposed | MTN, VOD | Owner approval requested for the proposed family. Existing general replays carry their original labels. This family starts with zero retained evidence. |

## Four steps

1. **Pick a share.** Show every watched share, including MTN, with search by code or issuer. A Radar handoff carries the instrument, product, timeframe and requested profile identity into the controls.
2. **Pick a hypothesis.** Show H1 and the families relevant to the share with their retained evidence status. Show Telecommunications for MTN and VOD as a proposed addition pending approval. Keep a hypothesis label distinct from the strategy that implements a particular test.
3. **Pick the strategy and version.** List supported registered identities and the exact product/timeframe, with date range and historical data available before submission. Offer eligible saved results first, and show a clear reason when the selected identity has no saved result or a runner is unavailable. An illustrative title is “MTN, jse_swing_3_5d v1.1.2, 4 May to 18 Nov, run 19 Nov”. Show the actual date basis and place the hash in Details.
4. **See the result.** Show queued/running/done/failed while the work runs. Lead with trade count, net result after stated costs, max drawdown, trades, sample-size warning, applied benchmark, observed date range and source-quality labels. Display the cost and fill assumptions beside the result. Link each trade to its trace and keep separate evidence for a completed run with zero trades, unavailable input, or an approximate profile.
