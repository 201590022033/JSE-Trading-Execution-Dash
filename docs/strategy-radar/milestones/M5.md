## Mission

Add a top-of-card manual decision plan using existing share and CFD estimators, with entry, quantity, stop trigger, stop-limit if applicable, trailing rule, target, holding horizon, cash or margin where supported, risk, itemised costs, break-even, projected gross and net. Inspect signed-in OST read-only via computer-use for actual order-field names and units, first cash shares and CFD; preserve evidence without submitting orders or paid quotes. Reuse 0.5%/R110 recorded share terms and CFD fee assumptions with provenance, not as universal verified rates; do not double count spread.
