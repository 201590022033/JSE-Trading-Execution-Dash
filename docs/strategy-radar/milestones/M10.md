# Strategy Radar M10 — six-choice radar and IRESS intraday qualification

Status: COMPLETED LOCALLY — owner-authorized parallel work alongside the existing research M5, 12 November 2026. This was a narrowly scoped exception to the usual one-active-milestone sequence; the research M5 scope and status are unchanged.

## Context

The current watch API retains Top 5, pinned and grace watches together. The
dashboard previously rendered every non-archived watch as a radar choice. A
read-only audit of 75 immutable Zebra ranking snapshots from 5–11 November
found eight chronological score-driving rank/input states, seven distinct
Top-5 sets and six Top-5 membership transitions. Repeated snapshots with the
same ranked inputs were not counted as new states. Some changes reflect new
completed-session closes, while others are relative rank changes among
competitors. The score uses 1-, 5- and 20-session returns and has no membership
hysteresis; this small audit does not justify changing its weights.

The owner chose a visible limit of the current Top 5 plus at most one manual
pin. Grace and other retained records stay in history, outside the live radar.
The owner also wanted a Zebra path for downloaded IRESS/ViewPoint intraday data.
The owner explicitly accepts this source for personal decisions and selected
an operator-accepted interpretation for the supplied MTN.JSE five-minute CSV:
prices in ZAR cents, Volume as shares, and UTC Date values as interval starts.
The original CSV and row values remain hash-bound; interpreted bars feed local
research shadow only, not orders or an assertion of a current quote.

## Scope and guardrails

- Limit visible radar choices to up to five current `in_top5` watches plus one
  pinned watch not already in the Top 5.
- Enforce at most one manual pin across the durable watch store. Reject a
  second pin explicitly; never silently replace the existing pin.
- Preserve all watch rows and append-only lifecycle history. Show non-radar
  retained watches in a collapsed, non-selectable history section. Do not
  change the two-session grace or ranking-score behavior.
- Audit and document the immutable source snapshots before considering any
  membership-stability policy. Do not infer that every rank change is a market
  move: compare latest session, score-driving inputs and competing candidates.
- Import manually supplied IRESS CSV exports under explicit operator consent
  for the selected interpretation. Preserve raw bytes and hashes alongside
  normalized values and the convention used. Manual imports can feed local
  research shadow; they do not enable unattended collection.
- Login remains user-operated. Do not receive credentials, automate a
  signed-in browser, defeat suspension, or add unattended polling/keepalive.
  Use the explicitly accepted convention for local research; it does not make
  an imported historical bar a live quote or authorize unattended collection.

## Current audit findings

The first retained snapshot is 5 November 06:04 UTC: NPN ranks 14 with score
53.1538 and data through 3 November. On 6 November 02:30 UTC, NPN is rank 15
with the same inputs while other candidates have newer sessions. At 05:35 UTC,
NPN's data reaches 5 November and its one-session return is 10.36%; its score
becomes 89.4459 and rank 1. The next distinct state on 6 November has data
through 6 November, a -2.56% one-session return and score 73.2194; NPN moves
to rank 6. On 9 November it remains on the 6 November input and score but
returns to rank 5 as competitors move. Newer NPN sessions then move it to rank
6 on 10 November (score 77.8324) and rank 5 on 11 November (score 80.0091).
Thus some falls are real input changes and some are relative displacement;
there is no evidence here that prices uniformly change too quickly or that
weights should be altered.
