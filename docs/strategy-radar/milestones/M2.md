## Mission

Connect canonical Top 5 and operator-selected research contenders to a durable watch set with strategy id/version, sector/benchmark and pin/unpin. Start a shadow watch and prewarm retained daily Zebra as soon as the share enters; do not wait for a volume trigger. Reconcile Top 5 changes idempotently, retain old decisions, allow a configurable 2-trading-session grace period, and keep pinned or active watches. Trigger bounded provider refresh through existing daily source paths with actual acquisition receipts; keep local historical research usable even if OST/IRESS login or one provider fails. Surface data coverage and source-specific problems per watch. Default watch scope is top five plus pins, not all instruments.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Top 5 add/change/remove, grace, pin, restart and duplicate runs behave deterministically; old decisions remain.
2. A usable partial/mixed-source daily history is made available without a complete provider dataset; unrelated missing fields do not block it.
3. Failed or unavailable OST/IRESS leaves other daily paths functioning; real source status is reported without paid requests.
