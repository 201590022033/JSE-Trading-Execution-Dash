### Why seven saved results carry cutoffs after 19 November

All seven records carry the basis RECONSTRUCTED_REPLAY_AS_OF_NOT_WALL_CLOCK. The legacy Lab and Zebra v2 runners choose the maximum bar availability time and add eight days, then store that simulated as-of time in the historical field named created_at. The observed-history converter models availability at the next UTC day. Thus a final observed session of 18 November can have an assumed availability of 19 November and a replay cutoff of 27 November. The time extension lets the existing replay engine reach the pending-order expiry horizon. The saved fields establish the latest input session and the configured replay clock. Actual execution timestamps for these records were unverified. Details now explains the clock basis.
