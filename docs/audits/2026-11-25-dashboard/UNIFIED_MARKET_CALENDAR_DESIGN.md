# Unified Market Event Calendar — design only

The live News & SENS workspace exposes headlines, Research Calendar, Anticipatory Radar, event preparation and retained advisory outputs separately. January and March 2027 SARB cards showed date anchors, USDZAR/ZAR_RATES, automated attribution and a SHA-256. Some source freshness warnings were visible. The Radar API's 343 SENS, zero Hermes and zero news leads describe its observed read window, not total database retention. Event preparation returned 28 entries and made no model calls. Same-day linked narratives or complete archives cannot be inferred from those numbers.

Use a composed read model over the existing calendar, SENS, news, corporate-action and radar services. Preserve original event and receipt IDs, official confirmation, evidence classification, exact release time if present, date-only anchors, ingestion clocks, source hash, revisions and model-attribution fields. For archive access, use deterministic bounded queries and visible coverage/truncation. Confirm per-route store ownership and bridge delivery separately. Do not merge stories by headline or overrule official dates with inferred ones. Group evidence only when identities support it, retain conflicts and link the original.

- Month/week render horizontal multi-day bars with continued-edge marks and overflow expansion. Day shows timed points and separate all-day/date-only rows. Agenda lists the same records chronologically.
- Use category colour plus icons/text for accessibility. Use distinct styles for confirmed dates, anticipated dates, and AI observation windows. An observation window is analytically separate from an actual duration.
- Include SARB/Stats SA and other existing macro events, SENS, results/trading statements, news catalysts, radar watches and corporate actions when supported by retained sources. Provide category, watchlist and sector filters plus missing-mapping indicators.
- Event details show publication versus capture versus scheduled clocks in SAST with UTC backing, source links and hashes, advisory analysis or abstention, revision history and portfolio-relevant research links. Date-only entries never gain a fictitious release time.
- Test overlap, month boundaries, exact timing versus date-only, revised dates, missing data, duplicate receipts, impact without a ticker, offset pagination avoidance, keyboard access and stale upstream feeds. Verify that an existing SARB ID and its evidence remain consistent across all four views.
